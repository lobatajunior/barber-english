# STREET ENGLISH — CONVERSACIONES BASE

## PERSONAJES
- Santiago (Santi) — hermano mayor
- Barbara (Barbi) — hermana menor
- Latinoamericanos aprendiendo inglés para crecer
- Tono: informal, real, humor natural entre hermanos

## USO
Estas 3 conversaciones son el ADN de Street English.
De cada una se extraen los 4 niveles: A1 → A2 → B1 → B2.
Repetición intencional de estructuras para grabación cerebral.
Cubren: saludos, despedidas, gustos, negaciones, aprobaciones,
rutina, transporte, direcciones, planes, futuro inmediato.

## CONVERSACIÓN 1 — "La Apuesta"

Santiago y Barbara están en la cocina. Santiago tiene su teléfono en la mano.

Santiago: Oye Barbi, te reto a algo.
Barbara: ¿A qué? Si me dices que limpies tu cuarto otra vez te juro que...
Santiago: No, no. Escucha. Desde ahora hasta que mamá llegue — solo inglés. El que hable español paga la cena.
Barbara: (se ríe) Santi, tú no sabes ni decir "hola" en inglés.
Santiago: I know how to say hello. See? Fácil.
Barbara: Eso fue trampa, mezclaste. Bueno, acepto. Empieza tú.
Santiago: Okay... (se pone serio) Hello. My name is Santiago. I am twenty-three years old. I live here. In this house. With you. Unfortunately.
Barbara: (en inglés, exagerado) Hello! My name is Barbara. I am twenty-one years old. I am smart. You are not.
Santiago: Hey! I am very smart. I am... (busca la palabra) ...how do you say "estudioso"?
Barbara: Hardworking. Or studious. But honestly? Neither fits you.
Santiago: Okay okay. I am hardworking. I work every day. I wake up early. I eat breakfast. I go to work. I come home tired. Very tired. Because I work hard.
Barbara: (sarcástica) Wow Santi, very impressive. I wake up early too. I go to school. I study English, math, history. I come home and I cook because somebody never cooks.
Santiago: I cook sometimes!
Barbara: When? When do you cook?
Santiago: I cook... on Sundays. Sometimes. When I want to.
Barbara: (se ríe) That is not cooking. That is making eggs.
Santiago: Eggs are food! Okay, new topic. What do you like? Tell me things you like.
Barbara: Okay. I like music. I like to dance. I like coffee in the morning. I like when you don't touch my things. I like weekends. I like pizza. I love pizza actually.
Santiago: Me too. I love pizza. I don't like vegetables. I don't like waking up early. I don't like my boss. He talks too much.
Barbara: Does he speak English?
Santiago: Yes. Too much English. Fast English. I don't understand him sometimes. It's embarrassing.
Barbara: That's why we're doing this, Santi. So you understand him. So you can answer back. So you don't just smile and nod.
Santiago: I know. I know. It's just... hard sometimes. You know?
Barbara: I know. Me too. But we're getting better. Look at us right now — we're doing it.
Santiago: Yeah... we are, aren't we? Okay. My turn. Do you have a boyfriend?
Barbara: Santi!
Santiago: What? It's English practice!
Barbara: I don't have a boyfriend. I don't want a boyfriend. I want to finish school, get a good job, travel, and maybe then—
Santiago: Okay okay, too much information. What about friends? Do you have good friends?
Barbara: Yes. My best friend is Valeria. She's funny. She's also learning English. We practice together sometimes. What about you? Do you have friends?
Santiago: Of course I have friends. I have many friends. They just don't... call much.
Barbara: (se ríe) Santi.
Santiago: It's fine! I prefer family anyway. Family is important. Our family is small but good. Mom works hard. Dad too. We are lucky.
Barbara: We are. I'm going to do something good with my life. I'm going to speak perfect English. I'm going to get a scholarship. I'm going to make them proud.
Santiago: Me too. I'm going to learn English. I'm going to talk to my boss without being scared. I'm going to ask for a raise.
Barbara: A raise? You've been there three months!
Santiago: In English! I'm going to ask in English and he won't be able to say no!
Barbara: (se ríe mucho) Okay Santi. I believe you.
Santiago: Do you?
Barbara: I do. I really do.

Silencio cómodo. Los dos sonríen.

Santiago: Hey Barbi?
Barbara: Yeah?
Santiago: I think we're going to be okay.
Barbara: (en voz baja) Yeah. I think so too.

Entra mamá por la puerta.

Mamá: ¡Hijos! ¿Qué hacen?
Santiago y Barbara: (al mismo tiempo, en inglés) Nothing!

Mamá los mira raro y se va a su cuarto.

Barbara: (susurrando) Did we just... speak English to mom?
Santiago: I think we did. So... who pays for dinner?
Barbara: Nobody. We both won.

## CONVERSACIÓN 2 — "El Interrogatorio"

Son las 8 de la noche. Santiago está en la sala viendo su teléfono. Barbara llega por la puerta.

Santiago: (sin mirar) You're late.
Barbara: Hello to you too, Santi.
Santiago: (ahora sí la mira, serio, en modo detective) Sit down, Barbara.
Barbara: Oh no. Are you serious right now?
Santiago: Sit. Down.
Barbara: (se sienta, se ríe) Okay okay. What?
Santiago: Where were you?
Barbara: I was at school.
Santiago: School ends at five. It is eight o'clock. Where. Were. You.
Barbara: I was with Valeria. We were studying.
Santiago: Studying where?
Barbara: At the library. Then we went to the park.
Santiago: The park. I see. And how did you get home?
Barbara: I took the bus.
Santiago: Which bus?
Barbara: The number seven. Same as always.
Santiago: And before the bus? How did you get to the park from the library?
Barbara: We walked. It's not far. Five minutes.
Santiago: Five minutes. Okay. And at the park — what did you do?
Barbara: We talked. We practiced English. We bought coffee from a street cart.
Santiago: You bought coffee. With whose money?
Barbara: My money! Santi, what is this?
Santiago: I'm practicing my English. You said I should practice more.
Barbara: (se ríe) This is not practicing, this is interrogating!
Santiago: It's the same thing. Okay, next question. Did you eat?
Barbara: Yes. I ate at school. A sandwich.
Santiago: Did you like it?
Barbara: No. I didn't like it. It was dry.
Santiago: I don't like dry sandwiches either. I don't like bad food. Life is too short for bad food.
Barbara: I agree. I completely agree.
Santiago: What do you like to eat after school?
Barbara: I like soup. I love soup actually. Hot soup when I'm tired. What about you?
Santiago: I like rice. I always like rice. I don't care what else — just give me rice and I'm happy.
Barbara: You're so simple Santi.
Santiago: Simple is good. Simple is honest. Okay, more questions.
Barbara: More?!
Santiago: Did Valeria take the bus too?
Barbara: No. She doesn't take the bus. She walks home. She lives near the library.
Santiago: She's lucky. I don't like the bus.
Barbara: Really? Why not?
Santiago: It's slow. It's crowded. I don't like crowded places. I don't like waiting either.
Barbara: Nobody likes waiting.
Santiago: Exactly. But you know what I like? I like when things are on time. I like order. I like knowing what happens next.
Barbara: That's why you're interrogating me right now.
Santiago: (se ríe) Maybe. Hey, was the bus on time today?
Barbara: No. It was late. Ten minutes late.
Santiago: See? I don't like that. Did you wait alone?
Barbara: No. There was an old woman at the stop. We talked a little.
Santiago: In English?
Barbara: (pausa) ...Yes actually. She asked me for directions.
Santiago: No way. Really?
Barbara: Really! She asked me how to get to the market. I told her — go straight, turn left at the corner, it's next to the pharmacy.
Santiago: And she understood you?
Barbara: She said thank you and smiled. So yes, I think she understood me.
Santiago: Barbi that's amazing. I'm serious. That's really good.
Barbara: I know! I was so nervous. My heart was going fast. But I did it.
Santiago: See? That's why we practice. For moments like that. Real moments.
Barbara: Exactly. Not for tests. For real life.
Santiago: For real life. I like that. I really like that.
Barbara: Me too. Hey Santi?
Santiago: Yeah?
Barbara: Are you hungry?
Santiago: I'm always hungry.
Barbara: Do you want rice?
Santiago: (sonríe) I love rice.
Barbara: I know you do. Come help me cook.
Santiago: I don't like cooking.
Barbara: I don't like cooking alone.
Santiago: ...Fair enough.

Los dos se van a la cocina.

Barbara: Hey — you were pretty good tonight. The interrogation thing.
Santiago: I know. I think I missed my calling.
Barbara: What do you mean?
Santiago: Maybe I should be a detective. An English-speaking detective.
Barbara: (se ríe) Sure Santi. Sure.
Santiago: I'm serious! I like asking questions. I'm good at it.
Barbara: You are actually. You really are.
Santiago: Thank you Barbi.
Barbara: Don't push it.

## CONVERSACIÓN 3 — "El Plan Perfecto"

Sábado por la mañana. Santiago y Barbara están en la sala. Los dos están aburridos.

Santiago: Barbi.
Barbara: What.
Santiago: I'm bored.
Barbara: Me too.
Santiago: Let's go out.
Barbara: Where?
Santiago: I don't know. You decide.
Barbara: No, you decide. It was your idea.
Santiago: Okay. Let's go to the mall.
Barbara: I don't like the mall on Saturdays. It's too crowded.
Santiago: You don't like the mall?
Barbara: I don't like crowded places. You know that.
Santiago: Okay okay. What about the park?
Barbara: Which park?
Santiago: The big one. Near the center.
Barbara: How do we get there?
Santiago: We take the bus. The number four goes there.
Barbara: How long does it take?
Santiago: Maybe twenty minutes. Not long.
Barbara: Okay. I like the park. I like being outside. Let's go to the park.
Santiago: Perfect. And after the park — food.
Barbara: Yes. I agree. I'm already hungry.
Santiago: You just woke up!
Barbara: I know. I'm always hungry in the morning. What do you want to eat?
Santiago: I want tacos.
Barbara: There are no tacos near the park.
Santiago: What? That's not possible. There are tacos everywhere.
Barbara: Not near that park. But there's a good sandwich place. On the corner, next to the pharmacy.
Santiago: I don't like sandwiches. You know I don't like sandwiches.
Barbara: You don't like dry sandwiches. These are different. They're good. I like them a lot.
Santiago: How do you know?
Barbara: I went with Valeria last week. I really liked it. The bread is soft, the meat is fresh—
Santiago: Okay okay, I believe you. Let's try it.
Barbara: Really?
Santiago: Really. I like trying new things. Sometimes.
Barbara: (se ríe) Sometimes. Okay, what time do you want to leave?
Santiago: Now?
Barbara: I'm not ready. Give me thirty minutes.
Santiago: Thirty minutes? For what?
Barbara: To get ready, Santi. I don't like going out looking like this.
Santiago: You look fine.
Barbara: Thank you but no. Thirty minutes.
Santiago: I don't like waiting.
Barbara: I know you don't like waiting. Twenty minutes then.
Santiago: Ten.
Barbara: Fifteen. Final answer.
Santiago: Fine. Fifteen minutes. I'm going to wait here. I'm going to be very patient.
Barbara: You're not going to be patient at all.
Santiago: You're right. I'm going to knock on your door in ten minutes.
Barbara: Santi!
Santiago: I'm going to say are you ready? Are you ready? Are you ready?
Barbara: (se ríe) You're impossible.

Quince minutos después. Están en la calle.

Santiago: See? I waited.
Barbara: You knocked twice.
Santiago: Twice is nothing. Okay — which way?
Barbara: We need to go straight. Two blocks. Then turn right.
Santiago: Turn right. Got it.
Barbara: The bus stop is on the corner. Next to the school.
Santiago: I see it. Let's go.

En la parada del bus.

Barbara: I hope the bus is on time today.
Santiago: Me too. I don't like waiting at bus stops. It's hot.
Barbara: It is hot. I don't like this heat.
Santiago: I don't like it either. But I like Saturdays. I really like Saturdays.
Barbara: Why?
Santiago: No work. No boss. No alarm. I like freedom.
Barbara: (sonríe) Me too. I love Saturdays.
Santiago: What do you want to do after lunch?
Barbara: I want to walk around. I like exploring.
Santiago: Exploring where?
Barbara: I don't know. That's the point. Just walk and see what's there.
Santiago: I like that actually. No plan. Just walk.
Barbara: Exactly. Sometimes no plan is the best plan.
Santiago: Who are you and what did you do with my sister? You always have a plan.
Barbara: (se ríe) Maybe I'm changing.
Santiago: Maybe. Hey — the bus is coming.
Barbara: Finally. I don't like waiting in the heat.
Santiago: Nobody likes waiting in the heat. Come on.

En el bus.

Santiago: (mirando por la ventana) I like this city.
Barbara: Really?
Santiago: Really. I like the streets. I like the people. I like the noise.
Barbara: I like it too. But I want to see other cities someday.
Santiago: Me too. I want to travel. I really want to travel.
Barbara: Where do you want to go?
Santiago: Everywhere. New York. London. Tokyo.
Barbara: Tokyo? Do they speak English in Tokyo?
Santiago: Some people do. And I'm going to speak English. So it's fine.
Barbara: (se ríe) You're going to walk into Tokyo speaking English.
Santiago: Exactly. And they're going to understand me. Because I'm going to be fluent.
Barbara: We're going to be fluent.
Santiago: Yes. We're going to be fluent. We're going to travel. We're going to see everything.
Barbara: I like that plan.
Santiago: Me too. It's the perfect plan.
Barbara: Better than the mall on a Saturday.
Santiago: Much better.

El bus para.

Barbara: This is our stop. Come on.
Santiago: (se levanta) Hey Barbi?
Barbara: Yeah?
Santiago: I'm glad we came out today.
Barbara: Me too Santi. Me too.

Bajan del bus. El sol está fuerte. Los dos caminan juntos hacia el parque.

Santiago: I'm hungry.
Barbara: We just got off the bus!
Santiago: I know. I'm still hungry.
Barbara: The sandwich place is five minutes away.
Santiago: Five minutes is too long.
Barbara: (se ríe) You're impossible.
Santiago: I know. But you like me anyway.
Barbara: (pausa) ...Yeah. I do.
